## Full description (Play: 4000 / App Store: 4000)

```
Payday shouldn't disappear by the 10th. Pesa helps you decide where every cedi of your salary goes — before you spend it.

PLAN YOUR SALARY INTO POTS
Give each part of your income a place to land: Rent, Food, Transport, Savings, Mom, Tithe, Emergency — whatever matters to you. Each pot has a monthly target, and Pesa shows how much of your salary is still waiting for a home.

RECORD AS YOU GO
• Send money to a pot with a few taps on the built-in keypad
• Log spending from a pot, or move money between pots
• Keep track of your bank and mobile-money balances by hand
• Add transactions for past months to complete your history

STAY ON TRACK
• Pace alerts warn you when a pot is on track to run over
• Bills pots remind you of their due day
• Savings and debt pots show your progress and the month you'll reach your goal
• Carry unspent money over to next month, or start fresh

SEE THE BIGGER PICTURE
• Insights: savings rate, a 6-month flow chart and a financial health score
• Net worth across your savings, emergency and debt pots
• Monthly Wrap: what you saved, gave and lived on, ready to share
• Projects: save toward big goals milestone by milestone

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Pesa never connects to your bank and never asks for bank logins or PINs
• No ads, no tracking, no selling your data
• Lock the app with a PIN
• Delete your account and all its data any time, right from the app

Pesa is a planning and record-keeping tool — it doesn't hold or move real money. Works great alongside your bank and mobile-money apps.

Also on the web at pesa.edtmsys.com — the same account on your phone and computer.
```
